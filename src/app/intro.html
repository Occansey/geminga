Geminga

gh’è minga — “it’s not there”

Geminga is a gamma ray pulsar that shows up in gamma rays and in almost nothing else. For twenty years nobody could find its counterpart at any other wavelength.

Astronomers in Milan named it for the pun in their own dialect: gh’è minga, which means it’s not there.
